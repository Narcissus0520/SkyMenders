# Generative AI Asset Policy

Generated assets may be evaluated only with a documented tool, model/service terms, prompt/process, date, human editor, references, and reviewer. Prompts must not request the style of a named living artist, commercial game, or identifiable brand. Outputs must be screened for recognizable characters, logos, copyrighted material, privacy issues, and license restrictions.

Generated development material is not automatically release-approved. It must pass the same provenance, originality, quality, accessibility, and professional review as other assets.
