# Privacy Policy Draft

Status: non-public engineering draft. It must not be published until the operating entity, contact details, effective date, server/data location, subprocessors, retention periods, youth/guardian treatment, statutory rights procedure, complaint channel, and current WeChat privacy-guide requirements are completed and approved under `EXT-003`.

The game uses the minimum information needed to create an account through WeChat login, synchronize progress and settings, resume expeditions, verify daily-challenge results, operate an anonymous leaderboard, secure the service, and process account deletion. Public rankings display only a server-generated system codename, an original robot avatar, score, completion time, turns and completion state.

The game does not request contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, album, phone number, custom avatar, free-text nickname, WeChat nickname/avatar, chat, region, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking identifiers. Login credentials and platform secrets are handled only by trusted servers. Users must have visible in-product routes to access, correct, export and delete account data. Deletion invalidates sessions and removes account-owned game data, subject only to an explicitly disclosed legal retention duty.

Operational and security logs use pseudonymous request or trace identifiers and must not contain login codes, OpenID, tokens, secrets, or full save payloads. Data transfers, storage locations, processors, retention and security controls must match `PRIVACY_DATA_INVENTORY.md` and the deployed environment before this draft can become effective.
