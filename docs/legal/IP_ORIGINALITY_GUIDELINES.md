# IP and Originality Guidelines

SkyMenders may use the generic turn-based projectile tactics genre but must remain independently expressed. Characters are rounded engineering robots in a floating-island maintenance setting, not worms or close analogues. Modules use construction, repair, gravity, magnetism, wind, foam, bridge, and energy language rather than recognizable names, silhouettes, sounds, lines, UI, maps, or promotional framing from another commercial game.

No competitor screenshots, assets, logos, fonts, music, audio, copied layouts, reverse-engineered files, or unauthorized fan works may enter the repository or release package. “Tribute” does not replace a license. Similarity concerns require written review before publication.
