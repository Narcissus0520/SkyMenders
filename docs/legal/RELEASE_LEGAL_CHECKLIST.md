# Release Legal Checklist

Before WeChat submission, qualified owners must confirm current publishing/approval/filing requirements, entity qualifications, anti-addiction/real-name integration, age rating, privacy guide, user agreement, privacy policy, access/correction/export/deletion, SDK inventory, server/data location, customer support, complaints, incident response, trademark, copyright, asset licenses, fonts/audio, public marketing claims, and platform rules.

Free distribution and absence of in-app purchases do not waive game publishing, privacy, or platform obligations. The repository provides engineering controls and evidence; it does not replace qualified legal or publishing review.
