# User Agreement Draft

Status: non-public engineering draft pending qualified legal/publishing approval under `EXT-003`.

The agreement must identify the operating entity, service and support contacts; describe eligibility and guardian consent; incorporate the current game approval, anti-addiction and real-name obligations applicable at launch; explain account security, service availability, acceptable conduct, intellectual-property ownership, update and termination handling, complaint and dispute channels, governing law, effective date and material-change notice.

Product-specific commitments are: no advertising, in-app purchases, paid pass, loot box, cash transaction, player trading, free-text nickname or chat in V1; no ownership right in temporary expedition state; deterministic daily results may be rejected when replay verification fails; system codenames may be reset for safety; account deletion is available from the product and removes player-owned data after authentication and confirmation.

This draft is an implementation checklist, not approved terms. No UI or marketing surface may label it effective until every placeholder is replaced, the policy versions are immutable, the user can view them before consent, consent evidence is stored when legally required, and counsel signs the release checklist.
