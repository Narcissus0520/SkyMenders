# PvE Expedition Content

## Regions

1. 云隙前站 introduces cloud soil, basic repair, bridges, and the 裂岩钻机.
2. 极冠铸场 combines alloy, crystals, magnetic transport, and the 极冠磁塔.
3. 翠风升流 combines elastic moss, wind, rescue, gravity changes, and the 倒悬控制器.
4. 坠核中枢 combines all materials and task pressures before the 失控浮岛主机.

Each region owns four validated map templates, at least four enemy prototypes, six ordinary node categories, region-specific wind and gravity, two selectable nodes, and one mechanism Boss.

## Content minimums

The `0.1.0` pack contains six robots, eighteen modules with two mutually exclusive routes each, eight enemy prototypes plus the Phase 4 elite whitelist, four three-stage Bosses, eighteen objective definitions, sixteen maps, twelve events, forty-six reward definitions, four workshop services, eighteen achievements, forty compendium entries, and six tutorials.

## Fairness and failure

Task-critical interactions cost no team energy. Every map declares all required capabilities as either squad-independent basics or map-provided capabilities. Node recovery restores 15% maximum health to active robots and 35% to robots that entered recovery state, while structural damage decreases only partially. All three robots entering recovery state ends the expedition. A node can be restarted once and the deterministic seed, map, opponents, event, hidden objective, and reward lock remain unchanged.

Research comes primarily from first discoveries, completion, and hidden engineering goals. Unlocks add choices only; there is no permanent combat-stat growth.
